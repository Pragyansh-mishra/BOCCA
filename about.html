OUR HERITAGE

"Cucinare è un atto d’amore, condividere il cibo è un atto di famiglia."

Founded in the quiet alleys of Rome in the winter of 1993, Da Maria began with just six wooden tables, a stone flour mill, and Nonna’s handwritten recipe notebook. Over three decades later, our pots still simmer at dawn, our bread is pulled warm from the stone hearth, and our door remains wide open for every traveler seeking the authentic taste of Italy.

THE WOOD & WHEAT

F

rom the volcanic soils of Campania to the ancient olive groves of Puglia, our pantry is sourced directly from independent growers. We believe the soul of Roman cuisine lies in restraint: when the olive oil is pressed this week, the tomatoes ripened on the vine, and the pecorino aged on pine shelves, every plate needs nothing more.

We do not follow culinary shortcuts or industrial machinery. Our pastas are extruded slowly through artisan bronze dies, creating a porous surface that catches every drop of rich sauce.

Philosophy

SLOW FOOD,
SACRED MOMENTS

Dining in Italy is not simply eating; it is an unhurried ritual of friendship, lively conversation, and shared memories that outlive the evening.
