About BOCCA

"Good Food. Great Coffee. Better Moments."

Welcome to BOCCA Italian Coffee & Pizza House, a café destination in Dungarpur for people who enjoy good food, coffee, refreshing drinks, and sweet treats. Explore a menu featuring pizzas, pasta, burgers, sandwiches, handcrafted-style coffee beverages, milkshakes, and desserts.

What We Offer

The BOCCA Experience

A welcoming spot for casual meetups, family dinners, sweet cravings, and daily coffee breaks in Dungarpur.

🍕 Artisan Pizza & Pasta

From our signature Bocca Special Pizza to classic Margherita, Paneer Tikka, Truffle Mushroom, and creamy Alfredo and Arrabbiata pastas, every recipe is made to satisfy with rich flavors and hearty toppings.

☕ Handcrafted Coffee & Frappes

Whether you need an eye-opening morning Americano, a silky Spanish Latte, a Honey Cinnamon Cappuccino, or an indulgent Nutella Frappe, our beverage bar offers coffee crafted to perfection.

🍔 Burgers, Wraps & Bites

Craving something quick and flavorful? Dive into our crispy Dirty Nachos, Peri-Peri Fries, loaded Cheese Burgers, Paneer Wraps, and open-faced toasted baguettes with garlic and basil.

🍰 Cakes, Shakes & Desserts

Sweet moments made memorable with our thick Hot Chocolates, Lotus Biscoff and Ferrero Rocher shakes, and freshly baked cakes including Black Forest, Biscoff Cheesecake, and Dark Chocolate pastries.
